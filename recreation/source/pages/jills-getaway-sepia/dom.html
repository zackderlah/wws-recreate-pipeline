Jill's Getaway Sepia

A4 $79 21cm x 30cm
A3 $159 30cm x 42cm
A2 $248 42cm x 60cm

Jill enjoys a wine and a good book when she sits on the verandah of this quaint cottage, it provides a peaceful atmosphere with its gardens and birds singing in the trees. Jill’s getaway is a modest version of the Queenslander with its timber-framed structure, elevated above the ground on stumps ideal for the sloping block in her street.

 

The house avails itself of cool breezes and ventilation beneath the floor, acting as a natural cooler on the hot summer days. A cosy cottage for Jill to reside in, as she tends to her hobbies of gardening and sitting on the verandah with a wine as she watches her neighbours passing by.
